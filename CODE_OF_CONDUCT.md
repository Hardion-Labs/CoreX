# Code of Conduct

CoreX aims to provide a respectful, constructive environment for everyone who participates in project spaces, including issue trackers, pull requests, code reviews, and other project activities.

This policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss technical disagreements constructively and focus criticism on ideas and work, not people.
- Accept good-faith feedback and act with empathy.
- Respect differing backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Keep project discussions relevant and professional.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption, sexualized attention or language, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable. Threats, sustained unwanted contact, and other conduct that makes project participation hostile are also prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in CoreX project spaces and when a person is representing the project or its community in public. Project maintainers may clarify whether conduct in another setting affects participation in CoreX.

## Reporting and enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this policy. To report a concern, use GitHub's available reporting tools or contact a maintainer through a communication channel that the maintainers have explicitly made available. This repository does not currently declare a private conduct-reporting address or other dedicated confidential channel. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect the privacy of involved people as far as practicable, and take action they consider appropriate. Possible responses include a warning, a request to stop or correct behavior, temporary restrictions, or removal from project spaces. Serious or repeated violations may result in exclusion from the project.
